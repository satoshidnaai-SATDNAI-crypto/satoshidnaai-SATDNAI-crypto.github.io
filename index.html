Tokenomics and Tax Policy

To support the progress, development, and long-term sustainability of the project, the SATDNAAI token applies a very low and fully transparent 0.5% transaction tax. This tax is designed to be almost unnoticeable for users and does not create pressure on buying, selling, or natural price growth.

  • Total Tax: 0.5%
  • Buy Tax: 0.5%
  • Sell Tax: 0.5%
  • Transfer Tax: 0%

This tax is used only for the technical development of the project, ecosystem growth, and maintaining the long-term continuation of the project.

• There are no hidden taxes.

• There are no non-transparent mechanisms in the contract.

• This tax is permanent, transparent, and will never be increased.
